30 days. No questions asked. If a product is not right for your business, email us within 30 days of purchase and we will refund you in full.

1. The 30-day window

Email us at tanushgargjanuary@gmail.com within 30 days of your purchase. Tell us you want a refund. You do not need to explain why.

2. What we refund

We refund the full purchase price you paid. We do not deduct a handling fee.

3. How the money comes back

Refunds are issued by Paddle.com, the Merchant of Record for your order. The refund goes back to the card or account you paid with. Your bank usually shows it within 5–10 business days.

4. After a refund

Please delete the files you downloaded. Your licence ends when the refund is issued, so you should not keep using or sharing the product.

5. Before you ask for a refund, ask us

If the product is not doing what you need, email us first. A lot of problems are a setup question we can answer in one reply, and you keep the tool.

6. When this does not apply

The 30-day refund does not cover a product bought to resell, or a purchase made in breach of our Terms of Service.

7. Contact

Email tanushgargjanuary@gmail.com. We answer support emails ourselves, and Paddle handles the refund transaction.
