Privacy Policy

OperatorKit · Last updated

The short version. We do not run ads, we do not sell data, and we do not track you across other websites. Payments are handled entirely by Paddle, not by us.

1. Who is responsible

OperatorKit is the seller of the products on this site. Payments are processed by our online reseller Paddle.com, which is the Merchant of Record for all orders.

2. What we do not collect

  • No advertising or tracking cookies.
  • No analytics that build a profile of you.
  • No sale or sharing of your data with anyone.

3. What Paddle collects

When you buy something, you enter your payment details directly with Paddle. We never see or store your card number. Paddle processes your payment as Merchant of Record and handles the related record keeping. See Paddle’s own privacy notice for how they process that data.

4. What we receive

To deliver your product and support you, we receive from Paddle the details needed to complete the sale — typically your name, email address, and what you bought. We use this only to deliver the product, send your receipt and download link, answer support emails, and meet our legal and tax obligations.

5. Emails you send us

If you email us for support, we keep that email so we can answer you and help you if you write again. We do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask to join one.

6. How long we keep it

We keep purchase records for as long as we are required to for tax and accounting purposes. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you.

7. Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us and we will action it, except where the law requires us to keep a record (for example a tax record of a sale).
